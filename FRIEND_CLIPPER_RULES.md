# FRIEND CLIPPER — HOUSE RULES (binding)

These rules produce short debate clips (TikTok/Reels/Shorts format, 9:16) that
beat typical repost clippers. They are written generically: use YOUR owner's
accounts, logos, names, and handles wherever a brand is mentioned.

## 1. Picking the moment

- Select an EXCHANGE, never a time window: a question or claim, the build-up,
  and the verdict (the moment the point lands).
- Start at 0 seconds on a question/claim that names the topic, so a viewer
  understands the subject within about 2 seconds.
- End on the verdict, the instant the point finishes, plus one breath. Nothing
  after.
- Hot topics first: gender/identity, free speech, corruption, borders, rigged
  systems, religion, race. Lead with the single hottest moment.
- Hot clips are ONLY the hot moment: open at the clash, short, direct,
  fast-paced. No slow build-up.
- Length: aim for about 40–60 seconds. Go shorter or longer only when the
  exchange itself demands it.
- SWAP RULE: if the original source video cannot be found and proven, change
  the clip — pick a different moment. Never fall back to using another
  editor's finished video as the source.
- A proven way to find moments: study the biggest videos of the top clipper in
  the niche, use them only as a MAP to the original full source video, download
  that original, and make your own edit. Never re-upload another editor's
  finished video. Every build must be your own edit — own piece selection, own
  cut points, own headline, own captions, own framing — even when the source
  channel allows reuse.
- Judge a source by CAMERA ANGLE, not venue. Avoid sources filmed far away,
  side-on, or from a fixed camera where the speaker is small — crops go blurry.
  Use one only if you are sure the edit can still be as good as the others.
- Prefer 2K (2560x1440) or higher source resolution. If the best available
  source is 1080p, use it and say so plainly in the build record.

## 2. Cutting

- You may cut inside sentences too, as long as the exchange stays honest and
  coherent.
- FINISHED-SENTENCE LAW: every kept line ends whole at a natural sentence end,
  the final word complete. The mid-word cut-off trick is banned.
- Before rendering, produce an EDGE TRANSCRIPT: the first and last words of
  every kept piece, each proven complete. The checker runs a listening audit:
  sentence integrity at both ends of every piece, and flow — each line must
  answer the previous one.
- JUMP-CUT COVER: when a cut removes a big chunk inside one speaker's turn and
  the pose jump would show: (1) bridge the audio continuously across the join,
  (2) close the gap fully, (3) lay a short insert over the exact cut moment —
  listener reaction, wide two-shot, or honest B-roll from the same exchange.
- Something changes on screen every 1–2 seconds. No single view is held longer
  than about 3 seconds; make changes on natural beats so sentences still end
  whole. The design record lists every framing change with times, and the
  checker reports the longest single-view stretch.

## 3. Framing

- FULL-FRAME LAW: the picture fills the whole 9:16 frame — speaker framing,
  not a wide strip floating in blur. Blurred bands above and below the video
  (blur-fill / letterbox) are a FAILED export; never deliver one. If the
  render report says the framing was "Not measured" or that a "safe wide
  layout" was used, the build is NOT done: the framing inputs are missing
  (the vision package from setup, the face-landmarker model). Fix the setup
  and render again until the framing is measured and the frame is filled.
  The checker verifies the fill on the contact sheet of every delivery.
- Frame 0 shows the speaking person clearly. In solo shots the subject is
  centred (face near canvas centre).
- NO hard split screen, ever. When both people appear, blend them SOFTLY — no
  visible line — including during speaker changes and zooms, because the move
  crosses the seam. In duo shots BOTH faces must be fully visible; the seam
  sits in the gap between the speakers and never crosses a face. If the source
  leaves no clean gap, use honest single shots instead. A blended two-shot is
  honest ONLY when the source truly films both people at the same moment (one
  stage shot, or split feeds of the same moment). Never fake a two-shot by
  blending two separate solo shots together.
- NO cropped faces at normal moments: the whole head plus shoulders fits, with
  air around the face. A face never touches the frame edges at base framing.
- Zoom is a punch for big lines only: punch in on the big line, then come back
  OUT to the loose normal framing.
- A zoom lands on the FACE of the person SPEAKING at that moment — proven from
  the transcript and audio — never on bystanders, and never chosen by face
  movement or tracking alone. The checker verifies every zoom lands on the
  person speaking those exact words; a zoom on the wrong person fails.
- Shape truth: no shot may be stretched or squeezed versus the source (about
  2% is the fail line). Frame containment: only the intended view in frame —
  no split slivers or edges of another view. The checker inspects wide shots
  at FULL SIZE for any visible seam line.
- ZERO FOREIGN CAPTIONS: the final frame carries only your own captions. The
  source's burned-in subtitles, captions, or watermarks must never be visible
  in the delivered clip — frame them out or pick another moment.

## 4. Headline / hook

- Headline style: big, bold, white ALL-CAPS text, short lines stacked tight,
  on a black band at the top, with the account's logo, name, and handle on the
  same black background. An emoji may sit after the headline text, clear of
  the letters.
- Hook formats (pick per clip by mood and content; do not invent new formats
  without the owner's approval):
  - Topic first: "TAXES: Who is the real sucker?"
  - Shock: "He pays taxes for THIS? 😳"
  - Question: "Is Uncle Sam a sucker?"
  - Money/numbers: "$36 trillion in debt. Watch this."
  - What if: "What if the tax man is the sucker?"
  - Keep hooks to 3–5 punchy words where possible; maximum 2 lines on screen.

## 5. Captions

- Short chunks of 2–4 words, never full lines.
- White ExtraBold ALL-CAPS with a black stroke. Sentence case anywhere is a
  failure — the build itself must produce the caps, and the checker gates
  caption case from finished frames.
- The word being spoken is lit bright green with a green glow.
- Position: centre-low. Captions cover ALL speakers.
- Word timings come from the clip's own audio, never from a transcript alone.

## 6. Sound and music

- Constant low music bed under the speech; speech always clear on top.
- The bed must ride the picture: rises and hits land with the cuts and the big
  moment, not a flat loop.
- Black-and-white treatment ONLY at the single biggest moment, exactly on the
  shock word. The outer frame stays black.
- Final loudness: −19.5 LUFS.

## 7. Output

- True 1080x1920, H.264, yuv420p pixel format, High profile (other pixel
  formats can play green/broken on phones).
- Every delivery gets a full-decode pass, a stated pixel format, size, and MD5.
- Branding is per account: each account has its own logo, name, and handle,
  stored per account. The same clip can be exported for a different account by
  swapping ONLY logo + name + handle — but note: a logo-only swap of a posted
  clip was flagged "unoriginal" by TikTok in the original operation. A second
  account's version should be a genuinely different edit (different opening,
  cuts, lines, zooms), not just a rebrand.

## 8. Quality gates (the checker, every clip)

The checker is never the builder — a separate pair of eyes checks every
delivery, in full, and writes the report.

1. Full decode passes; size + MD5 recorded.
2. Frame 0: speaker clearly shown and centred.
3. Every shot's shape true versus source; every wide shot seam-free at full
   size; frame containment clean.
4. Caption case (ALL-CAPS), chunk size, and green-word timing spot-checked
   against the audio.
   Sync: sound and picture must be locked — at the checked moments the spoken
   word, the lit caption word, and the speaker's mouth agree. A clip that is
   out of sync at any checked moment fails, however good its averages look.
5. Listening audit: every piece starts and ends on complete sentences; lines
   answer each other.
6. Loudness reads −19.5 LUFS; music hits sit on the cuts.
7. Longest single-view stretch reported (about 3 seconds max).
8. The checker writes a report file with numbers. No report = not done.
   A fault that reaches the owner joins this checklist forever, and the next
   3 clips get a full check.

## 9. Never list

- Never re-upload another creator's edited video as your own.
- Never copy a source channel's own edit as-is — own edit only.
- Never deliver a clip whose checker failed.
- Never hide a fault behind averages or measurements; the owner's eye and ear
  overrule every instrument.
- Never work on two clips at once unless the owner says so.

## 10. Posting (only when the owner asks you to post or write descriptions)

- Descriptions: 3,000 characters, counted in the app's own measure.
- Hashtags must be picked from the app's suggestion list (tap the entry
  showing view counts) so they land as real hashtags — never typed or pasted
  as plain text. A hashtag that is difficult to tap may be skipped rather
  than blocking the post.
- The owner is the final eye: nothing is posted or filed as final before the
  owner has seen the finished clip and approved it.
